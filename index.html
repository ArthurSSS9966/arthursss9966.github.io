01Doctoral researchFirst-author publication
Scientific Reports, 2025

Decoding movement beyond the motor cortex

What can human insular activity tell us about intended hand movement?

Built the lab's first complete deep-learning workflow for directional hand-movement decoding from human insular sEEG, connecting preprocessing, high-gamma feature extraction, recurrent models, and evaluation.

Alongside decoding, I investigated task-related neural activity using spectral analyses and latent-dynamics methods, including dPCA and GPFA. The goal is to connect discriminative performance with interpretable features of neural activity.

  • sEEG
  • LSTM
  • High-gamma features
  • Latent dynamics
Read the paper ↗
02Synaptrix LabsIndustry internship

Connecting live EMG to a silent-speech decoder

Got a state-of-the-art silent-speech model training on company infrastructure. I brought the MONA LISA EMG-to-text pipeline (Benster et al., 2024) up on the company's cloud GPU, tracking down out-of-memory failures with memory profiling and per-batch cache clearing.

Built a real-time EMG streaming client that records eight-channel facial and throat EMG from an OpenBCI board, filters it, and streams it over WebSockets to the team's Dockerized decoding server, and extended the server with streaming endpoints. Board-to-server streaming was tested end to end.

  • EMG
  • PyTorch Lightning
  • WebSockets
  • Docker
03FALCON benchmarkCross-session adaptation

Neural decoding across recording sessions

Implemented and compared Wiener, CEBRA, NDT2, and SwapVAE approaches in a consistent training, calibration, and testing workflow for neural-to-EMG decoding from NWB-format primate motor-cortex recordings.

Evaluated representation learning and few-shot adaptation, including encoder-frozen calibration and linear-head refitting, to investigate how decoders adapt to session-to-session variation.

  • SwapVAE
  • Few-shot adaptation
  • PyTorch
  • NWB
04Collaborative researchIEEE IJCNN, 2024

Regularized neural decoding with limited data

Co-designed a bidirectional GRU and attention decoder with a latent-dynamics front end for hand-velocity regression from macaque M1/PMd intracortical recordings on MC_Maze. Investigated mixtures of parameter-prior regularizers for learning from limited neural data.

Co-author and corresponding author of Efficient Neural Decoder: Mixture-Regularized Bidirectional GRU with Attention.

  • GRU + attention
  • Regularization
  • MC_Maze

Additional research directions

Seizure-onset detection

Developed a multi-loss approach combining seizure, anatomical, and temporal objectives, with artifact-aware preprocessing and clinician-marked onset-zone references. The CV-reported evaluation includes two participants.

Stimulation and memory

Analyzed intracranial recordings from a nine-participant hippocampal-stimulation memory study using spectral power, phase-amplitude coupling, and connectivity, with region-specific analysis and stimulation-artifact controls.
